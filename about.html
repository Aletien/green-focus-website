Our Story

Who We Are

Green Focus Organization (GFO) is a community-driven organization established on 5 December 2024 in response to the growing environmental, climate, humanitarian, and community challenges affecting the Rwenzori Region of Uganda, particularly Kasese, Bundibugyo, Ntoroko, and Bunyangabu.

GFO was founded through the initiative of Muhindo Herman, Kule Michael, and Baluku Frendick, who recognized the need for locally led and sustainable solutions to the region’s increasing exposure to natural disasters, environmental degradation, climate change, and its effects on community livelihoods and peaceful coexistence.

The organization works with communities, local leaders, development partners, technical experts, and other stakeholders to develop and implement practical solutions that protect the environment, strengthen community resilience, and promote sustainable livelihoods and peaceful coexistence.

Our Direction

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To develop and implement locally led, practical, and sustainable solutions that protect the environment, strengthen community resilience, and improve livelihoods in the Rwenzori Region.

Our Vision

A resilient Rwenzori Region where communities live in peaceful coexistence with a protected environment, sustainable livelihoods, and strengthened local capacity.

Our Values

Community leadership, environmental stewardship, inclusion, transparency, partnership, sustainability, and peaceful coexistence.
