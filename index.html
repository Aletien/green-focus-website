Who We Are

Locally Led Environmental and Climate Action in the Rwenzori Region

Green Focus Organization (GFO) is a community-driven organization established on 5 December 2024 in response to growing environmental, climate, humanitarian, and community challenges affecting the Rwenzori Region of Uganda, particularly Kasese, Bundibugyo, Ntoroko, and Bunyangabu.

GFO was founded through the initiative of Muhindo Herman, Kule Michael, and Baluku Frendick, who recognized the need for locally led and sustainable solutions to natural disasters, environmental degradation, climate change, livelihood challenges, and peaceful coexistence.
